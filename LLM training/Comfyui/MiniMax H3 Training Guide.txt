* **Checkpoints:** `sam3.1_multiplex_fp16 (1).safetensors` -> `ComfyUI/models/checkpoints/`

## Part 2: Creating a Proper Character Sheet for MiniMax H3
Before running a character swap, your reference sheet must be properly formatted so the model can accurately interpret multiple angles and details:

1. **Multi-Angle Layout:** Include front, left side profile, right three-quarter, and full back views of the character on a single clean canvas.
2. **Consistent Design:** Ensure clothing, hair color, eye color, and accessories are identical across all poses displayed on the sheet.
3. **Clean Background:** Use a neutral, solid-color background (preferably `#EDEFF2` seamless studio gray) to prevent unwanted background bleed during the mask and reference extraction steps.
4. **Detail Insets:** For subjects with tattoos or micro-features, add high-resolution close-ups of the face, throat, and sleeve details in a lower row.
5. **Style Fidelity:** Match the art style directly to what you want outputted, avoiding conflicting stylistic cues.

## Part 3: Workflow Step-by-Step Execution

1. **Load Workflow:** Load `MiniMax-H3-Character-Swap-Production.json` into your ComfyUI workspace.
2. **Upload Reference Assets:** 
   - Load your multi-angle character sheet into the **Picture** node (Node 12).
   - Load your target performance file into the **VHS_LoadVideo** node (Node 13).
3. **Configure Isolation Mask (Node 950):** 
   - **CRITICAL ANTI-NUDITY RULE:** Set mask text to `"face, neck, arms"`. Do NOT set to `"woman"`.
   - Setting to `"face, neck, arms"` ensures the original video's clothing is never erased or blacked out.
4. **Apply Master Production Prompt Template (Node 5):**
   ```text
   Use <Video_1> as the master scene and performance. Replace the face, hair, neck, and exposed arms of the woman in <Video_1> with the character from <Picture_1>, while strictly preserving her original outfit and clothing from <Video_1>.

   Maintain the exact outfit, clothing, garments, fabric textures, folds, and colors worn in <Video_1> completely unchanged. She remains fully clothed in the original garments from <Video_1>.

   Transfer the character's facial identity, hairstyle, facial features, throat/neck tattoos, and sleeve tattoos onto exposed arm skin from <Picture_1>. Maintain natural movement, correct lighting, and sharp tattoo details on exposed skin. Replace only the face, neck, and exposed arms; leave the clothing and background completely unchanged.
   ```
5. **Set Generation Parameters:**
   - **Megapixels Node:** Set output resolution tier (Default `1` yields ~768p / 768×1376 portrait 9:16).
   - **Resolution Selector:** Match target source aspect ratio (`9:16 (Portrait Widescreen)`).
   - **Duration Node:** Define output length in seconds (typically `4.0` to `5.0`).
6. **Queue Prompt:** Run the workflow (~3.8 to 7 minutes on RTX 5070 Ti with Turbo LoRA).

## Part 4: Mandatory DaVinci Resolve 4K SuperScale & Color Grade Pipeline
Raw ComfyUI generations render at 768p with soft facial micro-details and flat Rec.709 digital contrast. To achieve commercial broadcast standards, all video outputs must pass through DaVinci Resolve Studio 21.1.1:

1. **3× Neural SuperScale:**
   - Project Timeline resolution set to 4K UHD (2160×3840 for 9:16 portrait).
   - Enable `superScale: 3`, `superScaleSharpness: "1"`, `imageResizeMode: "sharper"`.
   - Reconstructs facial skin texture, iris definition, hair strands, and crisp tattoo line work from the 768p render.
2. **Color Grading & Beauty Presets:**
   - **Avoid Heavy Print Film LUTs (e.g. Kodak 2383):** These double saturation (from ~32 to 63) and crush shadows into muddy black.
   - **Preset 1 (Clean Studio Beauty 3D LUT):** Apply `MCP/Workhorse_Clean_Studio_Beauty.cube` (lifts shadows 1.5%, open 0.94 gamma for radiant luminous skin, natural 32.7 saturation).
   - **Preset 2 (Pure Natural CDL):** Offset +0.01 (open shadows), Power 0.99, Saturation 1.00 (100% authentic color).
3. **Hardware NVENC Render:**
   - Export via NVIDIA NVENC H.264/H.265 at Best Quality. Typical 5-second 4K render finishes in ~2.2 seconds.
   - Executable via: `python F:\WORKHORSE\pipeline\h3_davinci_postprocess.py <input_video.mp4>`.

## Part 5: Anti-Nudity & Garment Preservation Rules
When performing character swaps on clothed subjects:
1. **Never Mask `"woman"` Directly:**
   - Masking `"woman"` blacks out the entire silhouette (including clothes). MiniMax H3 will hallucinate new body parts from scratch.
2. **Mask `"face, neck, arms"` (Node 950):**
   - SAM3 only erases the head, neck, and exposed arm skin. The source video's original outfit, fabric folds, seams, and lighting remain 100% untouched.
3. **Explicit Clothing Lock in Prompt:**
   - Always include: *"Strictly preserve her original outfit and clothing from <Video_1> completely unchanged. She remains fully clothed in the original garments from <Video_1>."*

---

## Part 6: Tattoo Transfer Protocol
1. **Never Prompt Covered Tattoos:**
   - If the subject wears a shirt, do NOT prompt sternum, chest, or full back tattoos. Prompting covered tattoos forces the model to remove clothing to make the tattoos visible.
2. **Target Only Exposed Skin:**
   - Anchor prompt tokens: *"Transfer the character's facial identity, hairstyle, throat/neck tattoos, and sleeve tattoos onto exposed arm skin from <Picture_1>."*
3. **Multi-Angle Character Sheet Grounding:**
   - Ensure the reference sheet includes both full-body turnaround views and high-resolution macro crops of the tattoos on a seamless studio gray cyclorama (`#EDEFF2`).

---

## Part 7: Audio Stream Verification Requirement
MiniMax H3 and ComfyUI's `VHS_LoadVideo` node require an active audio track. If a reference video has no audio, ffmpeg will throw an extraction error.
- **Pre-flight Rule:** Check audio with `ffprobe`. If silent/missing, mux a silent AAC track before loading into ComfyUI:
  ```bash
  ffmpeg -y -i input.mp4 -f lavfi -i anullsrc=channel_layout=stereo:sample_rate=44100 -c:v copy -c:a aac -shortest output_with_audio.mp4
  ```
