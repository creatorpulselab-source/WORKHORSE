---

### 🎯 Platform-Optimized Captions
**One image. Five platforms.**  
Prism writes natively for Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, X, and general use — not a copy-paste job. Hook energy for TikTok. Desire-building for Instagram. Punchy for Twitter. Warm for Facebook.

---

### 🎨 Flux Image Prompts
**Your next AI render, already written.**  
Generate a detailed Flux.1/2 prompt from your photo — complete with pose vocabulary, lighting direction, and scene composition — ready to drop straight into your image generator.

---

### 🎬 WAN Video Prompts
**Turn a still into a cinematic clip.**  
Prism writes full WAN 2.1/2.2 video prompts from any image — motion direction, camera moves, atmosphere. Your static content becomes a motion brief in seconds.

---

### 📸 Pose Series Generator
**Six shoot-ready poses. One reference photo.**  
Upload one image. Get 6 unique pose and composition variations — with visual consistency rules locked in (face, tattoos, outfit, location stay constant; only the pose changes). Perfect for content batching.

---

### 🧵 X Thread Generator
**One idea. Five tweets.**  
Prism breaks your content concept into a structured 5-tweet thread built for engagement and saves — hooks, value, CTA, all in your voice.

---

### ⚡ Generate All Mode
**Everything, in parallel.**  
Hit Generate All and Prism fires caption, image prompt, video prompt, poses, and hashtag set simultaneously. Results pop in as each one finishes. One click. Full content package.

---

### 🤝 UGC & Brand Mode
**Sounds like you. Features the product.**  
Built-in UGC content type writes authentic first-person captions that feature the brand naturally — none of the "ad" energy that tanks UGC rates. Prism searches the brand automatically so it gets the product right.

---

### 🔍 Brand Research (Auto)
**Prism knows the brand before you type a word.**  
For product and UGC content, Prism uses live Google Search grounding to find the brand's real tagline, voice, and product info — then builds it into the caption. Paste a URL for even deeper accuracy.

---

### 🏷️ Tiered Hashtag Sets
**30 tags. 3 tiers. Real strategy.**  
High Reach (500k–5M), Engagement (50k–500k), and Niche (<50k) — not a random dump of popular tags. Analyzed from your actual image, not a keyword list.

---

### 🥋 Hook Strength Scorer
**Know if your hook will stop the scroll — before you post.**  
Prism scores your opening line 1–10 for curiosity gap, specificity, and pattern interrupt. If it's weak, you get 2 stronger alternatives you can copy in one click.

---

### 📈 Trend-Aware Grounding
**What's trending this week in your niche.**  
Prism searches Google in real time to surface what's actually trending for your niche and platform — then suggests caption angles you can build on right now.

---

### 📅 Content Schedule Queue
**Plan your feed. Own your calendar.**  
Queue up any generated post for a future date and time. Mark it posted when it's live. Your schedule feeds your streak, your streak feeds your strategy.

---

### 🔥 Posting Consistency Streaks
**Consistency is a skill. Track it.**  
Prism tracks your posting streak automatically from your history. No extra steps. Just keep posting.

---

### 📊 Analytics Dashboard
**Your numbers. Your patterns. Your best post.**  
Log likes, views, and comments on any post directly in Prism. See per-platform and per-mode breakdowns, a 30-day activity bar chart, and your single best-performing post at a glance.

---

### 🎯 Distribution & Feature Hub Engine
**Find the accounts that'll shoutout your content.**  
A searchable database of Instagram and TikTok feature-page hubs — filtered by niche, tier, and platform. See submission rules, required hashtags, and link to each profile. Auto-appends hub hashtags to your caption.

---

### 🖼️ Alt-Text Generator
**Accessible content. One click.**  
Prism generates a clean, screen-reader-friendly alt text description for any uploaded image. Under 125 characters, no filler boilerplate.

---

### 🔁 What-Worked Feedback Loop
**The AI learns what actually worked for you.**  
Rate your posted captions 1–5 stars. Prism pulls your highest-rated posts back into every future caption prompt as style reference. The longer you use it, the better it writes *your* voice.

---

### 💡 Content Ideas Engine
**Never stare at a blank content calendar again.**  
Prism surfaces upcoming holidays, seasonal moments, and evergreen themes. Type any idea keyword and expand it into a full caption, image prompt, or video prompt instantly.

---

### 🎤 Video Understanding (AI-Native)
**Prism watches your video. The whole thing.**  
Upload any MP4, MOV, or WEBM up to 500MB. Prism's AI watches the full video — motion, audio, dialogue — and generates captions and prompts from complete video understanding, not just a thumbnail.

## VISUAL DIRECTION BRIEF

### Color / Aesthetic Direction
- **Name:** "Prism" implies light refraction — spectrum, clarity, transformation
- **Palette suggestion:** Deep dark background (near-black or midnight navy) with prismatic accent colors — iridescent purples, electric blues, warm golds, and clean whites. Think "studio dark room with neon creative energy."
- **NOT:** Generic SaaS blue-and-white. NOT a generic gradient AI logo.

### Logo / Icon Concept Ideas
- A triangular prism refracting a beam of white light into a spectrum
- A camera aperture / iris that contains a color spectrum inside
- An abstract "P" formed by overlapping translucent color planes
- A geometric lens or crystal shape with internal color refraction

### Key Visual Themes (for ad creatives, feature screenshots, reels)
1. **The Upload Moment** — Drag-drop visual, image lands, immediate response. Fast. Instant.
2. **The Parallel Generate** — Multiple output cards "popping in" simultaneously. Feeling of abundance.
3. **Platform Rows** — Same image, 5 different captions laid out side by side — the differentiation story told visually.
4. **The Pose Grid** — 6 pose cards laid out in a grid. Visual proof of what Pose Series outputs.
5. **Mobile-First Wizard** — The 5-step flow on a phone screen. Clean, card-based, no clutter.
6. **Streaks + Analytics** — The gamification / progress layer. Makes creators feel growth is trackable.
7. **Before/After Caption** — Generic caption vs. Prism-generated caption, same image. Contrast sells itself.
